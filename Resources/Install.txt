AIR STATS

1. Drag Air Stats into Applications.
2. Open Air Stats from Applications and click its menu-bar item.
3. Choose Connect with Google and import your own Desktop app OAuth JSON.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Setup instructions: https://github.com/Texseractrum/air-stats#google-cloud-setup
Source and issues: https://github.com/Texseractrum/air-stats

Check the release notes for notarization status. The initial signed preview
is not notarized, so Gatekeeper may block first launch. After reviewing the
source and checksum, you can choose System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Open Anyway. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

Your imported configuration and tokens are stored in this Mac's Keychain.
No developer credentials, health data, or telemetry are bundled in this app.

Heart rate is the latest synced reading, not a live Bluetooth stream.
Google does not expose Fitbit Sleep Score or Daily Readiness through this API.
